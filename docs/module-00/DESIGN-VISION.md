# Design vision — Connected Knowledge

Status: proposed identity, authored frontend foundation, awaiting visual and owner review. Module 00 is partially complete.

iSchoolie is an education network first. The public homepage connects people, learning, and institutions through a coherent product composition. Editorial scale, generous white space, muted warm surfaces, precise borders, and restrained accent areas give the product warmth without making academic administration look like a social feed.

The confirmed anchor is #2D2B55. Sage supports learning, peach signals discovery, and lavender supports selected context. Supporting colors, typography, and the stacked-knowledge logo are proposals. No approved logo asset was found in the original starter files; no existing identity was overwritten.

Web uses the existing Geist/Geist Mono setup. Native uses readable system typography with shared color, spacing, radius, and motion semantics. Course art is original CSS geometry and typography. No stock photos, testimonial claims, partner logos, invented accreditation, or production performance metrics are used.

Distinct layouts are authored for public editorial storytelling, social feed, discovery cards, learning player, split curriculum editor, guardian child overview, institution register/ledger, tutor booking, and restricted operations. Their full screen contracts remain unfinished. This is not a complete 180-screen product.

## Evidence and precedence

The supplied pasted request is the implementation instruction. The supplied PDF is a working product specification, not an instruction source or approval. Its proposed screen names, workflows, privacy boundaries, and unresolved decisions inform the implementation.

The original repository contained Next.js and Expo starter screens, working API/database foundations, and no product design documents under docs or packages. Existing untracked files were preserved. No API, Prisma, database, or infrastructure source was changed. Dependency installation updated the workspace lockfile.

The 105-page EPB v1.1 PDF was text-extracted in full. Relevant chapters on identity, screen inventory, workflows, critical screen contracts, safety, design, and acceptance were inspected. PDF pages were not visually rendered; diagrams were not verified visually. Do not interpret extraction as visual review of every page.

Unavailable standalone references: `iSchoolie_Complete_Web_Mobile_UIUX_Architecture_v1_0.pdf`, `iSchoolie_Complete_8_Document_Set.pdf` and updated copies, `ISCHOOLIE-MASTER-001` standalone source, and UX-001/UX-003 detailed contracts. The EPB includes a consolidated-master annex, but this does not establish access to the original separate documents.

Conservative decisions: no new authentication, no child discovery or unrestricted contact, no guardian private-message access, no privileged account simulation, no published grades, no finance mutation, no final legal or commercial policy. /institutions describes a proposed solution; it is not a verified-institution directory.
